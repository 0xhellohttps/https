About HTTPS Entertainment

Where music meets
engineering discipline.

HTTPS is a development studio first. The entertainment arm applies the same people and the same standards to music: campaigns that are measured, releases that ship on time, and rails for shows and payouts that are audited before they go live.

We work for record labels and independent artists. A label gets a technical partner that can build and secure its infrastructure. An artist gets a team that handles the site, the release, the show, and the money, without hand-offs.

Out of Office

Date TBA · Location TBA

Out of Office is a PVP party showcasing proof of experience. In plain terms: a player-versus-player night where what you did on the floor earns an attestation that proves you were there and what you took part in.

Attestations are issued through the studio's identity and attestation work and tied to vyb ticketing, so the record of a night is verifiable, portable, and owned by the person who earned it.

  • Format: PVP
  • Record: proof of experience
  • Ticketing and settlement: vyb
  • Identity: studio attestation stack
